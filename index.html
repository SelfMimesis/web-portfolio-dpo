01 / MY PERSPECTIVE

Diego
Pérez Obrero.

Graphic designer for film and TV.
An artist’s way of seeing.

I combine creativity, technical skill and attention to detail to bring a production’s visual elements to life. My background in Fine Arts is the starting point: an artistic foundation that connects aesthetics and concept.

02 / FROM SKETCH TO OBJECT

Fiction you
can touch.

Small objects.
A whole world behind them.

I design props, posters and documents that belong within a story. My command of the Adobe suite lets me move from digital illustration to print design with precision.

I also create set sketches and pitch decks that help define the look and communicate the essence of each production.

03 / CRAFT AND MATERIAL

The mark
of the hand.

The character of a letter.
The texture of a surface.

Hand lettering and scenic painting are part of my toolkit. I use them to create signs, paintings, documents and bespoke graphic elements.

A way to give identity to the pieces that inhabit each set.

04 / SCREENS AND CODE

Stories
become interactive.

From graphic design
to digital tools.

Through an intensive six-month bootcamp funded by a Facebook scholarship, I trained in HTML, CSS, JavaScript and React.

I bring these skills into my practice to tackle the challenges of screens and new technologies on set, combining design and programming.

From Seville.
As a team.

the right connections.
